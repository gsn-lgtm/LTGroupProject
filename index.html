What we need from you

Please read the packet and tell us what to fix before we ask the Board to endorse it. We are not asking Leadership Trussville to pay for construction. We are asking the Board to stand behind the idea so our group can walk into City Hall with a finished plan.

  • Does the idea make sense?
  • Is Rank 1 (the pool campus on Parkway) the right first site?
  • Anything you would not want the Board to put its name on?

The idea in one paragraph

Trussville’s public storm refuge is four school rooms plus two St. Clair rooms the City does not control. The old Yarbrough community hall is gone. The city pool is closing after the 2026 season. Trussville 2040 already asked for park shelters and a place children with disabilities can play. Auburn built that playground (Town Creek). We want both pieces on the pool site the City is already redrawing: a rated hall of at least 3,000 square feet and a right-sized inclusive playground.
